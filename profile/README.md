# GlobeDoc Research Hub

GlobeDoc Research Hub is a non-profit research hub developing **GPOC – the Global Patient co-Owned Cloud**: a free, global health record that belongs to the patient, follows each person across borders and over a lifetime, and is built openly with researchers.

## Status
The GlobeDoc platform is under active development. The source code is being consolidated from several repositories and is kept private during development for security reasons. It will be released under an open-source licence as soon as it is safe to do so.

Our goal is recognition as a **Digital Public Good** under the Digital Public Goods Alliance standard.

## Get involved
Developers, clinicians, researchers and patients are welcome to contribute through our working groups:
https://globedoc.com/volunteer
