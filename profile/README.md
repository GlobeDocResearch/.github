# GlobeDoc Research Hub

GlobeDoc Research Hub is a Swedish non-profit research association developing **GPOC – the Global Patient co-Owned Cloud**: a free, global health record that belongs to the patient, follows each person across borders and over a lifetime, and is built openly with researchers.

## Open source

The GlobeDoc app is open source under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0: [globedoc-app](https://github.com/GlobeDocResearch/globedoc-app). It is a research prototype and not for clinical use or real patient data.

We have applied for recognition as a **Digital Public Good** under the Digital Public Goods Alliance standard.

## Get involved

Developers, clinicians, researchers and patients are welcome to contribute through our working groups: https://globedoc.com/volunteer
